# CDP Reporting Made Simple: Your Path to Climate Leadership

In today's climate-conscious world, environmental reporting has become a crucial aspect of corporate responsibility and sustainability leadership. CDP stands at the forefront of this movement, providing a comprehensive framework for environmental disclosure and assessment.

## What is CDP?

CDP, formerly known as the Carbon Disclosure Project, is a non-profit organization that oversees global environmental reporting for investors, companies, and regions. Recognized internationally as the "gold standard of environmental reporting", CDP's data plays a pivotal role in driving environmental action and monitoring progress worldwide.

<!-- ![CDP Overview](/assets/resources/case-studies/cdp-reporting-made-simple/cdp-overview.png) -->

The organization's journey began in 2000, building upon the Global Reporting Initiative's (GRI) framework, with the first submission taking place in 2002. Today, CDP's comprehensive approach encompasses three critical environmental aspects:
- Climate change impact
- Water management strategies
- Deforestation prevention measures

In 2021, CDP achieved a significant milestone with over 13,000 entities, including companies, cities, states, and regions, representing 64% of global market capitalization, disclosing their environmental data through the platform.

## Understanding CDP Scores

CDP scores serve as a comprehensive evaluation of an organization's environmental performance. These ratings are crucial for investors making informed decisions and for companies benchmarking their sustainability efforts.

<!-- ![CDP Scoring System](/assets/resources/case-studies/cdp-reporting-made-simple/score-diagram.png) -->

### CDP Score Breakdown

#### Leadership (A/A-)
- Industry leaders in environmental stewardship
- Demonstrated achievement of science-based targets
- Comprehensive water-related risk strategies
- Effective deforestation impact mitigation

#### Management (B/B-)
- Active environmental impact management
- Implemented positive environmental changes
- Clear sustainability strategies

#### Awareness (C/C-)
- Growing environmental consciousness
- Comprehensive impact evaluation
- Understanding of business operations' environmental effects

#### Disclosure (D/D-)
- Initial environmental reporting stage
- Basic information disclosure
- Foundation for future improvement

#### Failure to Disclose (F)
- No CDP submission for the reporting year
- Missed opportunity for environmental transparency
